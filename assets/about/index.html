About Me

I am a Unity Game Developer based in Lahore, Johar Town, with a background in Computer Science and a strong interest in interactive game experiences.

I enjoy taking a game idea and gradually turning it into something playable. For me, game development is not only about writing code — it is also about understanding how players interact with a game and making each system feel clear, responsive and enjoyable.

My work often involves building gameplay systems, player interactions, UI experiences and the supporting logic that brings different parts of a game together. I particularly enjoy solving development problems and finding practical ways to improve the final player experience.

I am continuously improving my Unity and C# skills while exploring better approaches to game architecture, optimization and polished mobile gameplay. My goal is to keep growing as a developer and create games that are simple to understand, enjoyable to play and memorable for the player.

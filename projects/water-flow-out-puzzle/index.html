About the Project

Water Flow Out Puzzle Game

Water Flow Out Puzzle Game is a calm and relaxing puzzle experience focused on connecting pipes and creating complete water-flow paths.

Players rotate and connect different pipe pieces to guide water through the board and reach the correct destinations.

The game combines pipe connection mechanics with color path matching and increasingly challenging puzzle layouts.

Each level encourages players to analyze the board, plan their moves and find the correct solution without relying on fast reactions.

Smooth water-flow effects provide satisfying feedback when the correct path is completed, while the clean interface keeps the gameplay simple and accessible.

About the Project

Development overview

Cash Cleaner Simulator is a Unity-based mobile simulation game focused on handling, cleaning, counting and organizing money.

The project includes interactive money handling, cash counters, washing machines, counting systems, item interactions and gameplay progression designed for mobile players.

I worked on the gameplay programming and interactive systems that connect the different machines, money objects and player interactions into a complete playable experience.

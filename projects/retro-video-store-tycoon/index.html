About the Project

Development overview

Retro Video Store Tycoon Sim is a Unity-based simulation project focused on creating a complete retro video store management experience for Android.

The project includes interactive customer systems, inventory management, store progression, gameplay interactions and simulation mechanics designed around running and expanding a video rental business.

I worked on the gameplay programming and systems required to connect the different parts of the simulation into a playable mobile experience.

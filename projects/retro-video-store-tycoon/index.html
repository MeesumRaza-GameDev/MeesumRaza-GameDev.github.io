About the Project

Development overview

Retro Video Store Tycoon Sim is a Unity-based Android simulation game focused on building and managing a retro video rental business.

I worked on the core gameplay systems including store management, customer interactions, inventory handling, product rentals, upgrades and business progression.

The project demonstrates my experience with Unity, C#, gameplay programming, UI/UX implementation, simulation mechanics and mobile game development.

The game was developed with a focus on creating a smooth and accessible mobile gameplay experience while maintaining a nostalgic retro visual style.
